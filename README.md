# Inverted-Pendulum-Linear-Control-Project


This project addresses the modeling, analysis, and control of an **inverted pendulum system**, a canonical problem in control engineering. The primary objective is to **stabilize the pendulum in its upright position** using classical linear control methodologies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The inverted pendulum is a **nonlinear, inherently unstable system** mounted on a cart capable of horizontal motion. The control goal is to maintain the pendulum upright while allowing free motion of the cart along a linear track.

The system’s nonlinear dynamics were first derived analytically and then **linearized around the equilibrium point** to obtain a state-space model suitable for classical control design and performance analysis.

---

## ⚙️ System Description

- **Components:** Pendulum, cart, and horizontal track.  
- **Control Objective:** Stabilize the pendulum in the upright position.  
- **Modeling Approach:**  
  - Nonlinear equations of motion derived using Newtonian or Lagrangian mechanics.  
  - Linearization around the upright equilibrium to obtain state-space representation.  

---

## 🧠 Analysis and Design Steps

1. **System Modeling and Linearization** – Derivation of nonlinear dynamics and linear approximation near the upright position.  
2. **Transfer Function & State-Space Representation** – Conversion of linearized model for control analysis.  
3. **Stability Analysis** – Examination of eigenvalues and pole locations.  
4. **Frequency-Domain Analysis** – Root locus, Bode, and Nyquist plots for controller design insight.  
5. **PID Controller Design & Tuning** – Implementation of classical proportional-integral-derivative control.  
6. **Performance Evaluation** – Assessment of settling time, overshoot, and steady-state error.

---

- Linearization techniques for nonlinear dynamical systems.  
- Application of classical control methods to real-world systems.  
- PID controller design and tuning for stability and performance optimization.  
- Time- and frequency-domain analysis of control systems.
